Zyloh — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Zyloh is an offline habit-tracking and life-coaching app. Your privacy is simple: Zyloh does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

What data does Zyloh use?

  • Everything you enter in the app (your name, habits, scores, history) is saved only on your own device.
  • Zyloh has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that store your information.
  • The app works fully offline. It does not send your data anywhere.

Backups

You can export a backup file from the app. That file contains your own habit data and stays under your control. You can delete it at any time.

Internet usage

Zyloh only loads its own resources to display the app. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Children

Zyloh does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, it will be updated on this page. Since the app itself stores no data, future changes will not affect data already on your device.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing.
